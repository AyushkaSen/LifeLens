Privacy & Methodology

Understanding local-first data guarantees and behavioral modeling.

security Local-First Privacy Architecture

LifeLens processes personal behavioral, productivity, and nutritional data strictly on your local device. In Phase 1, all records are stored in a local SQLite database (lifelens.db). No personal data, task details, or dietary logs are transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

dataset Static USDA Nutrition Reference

Nutritional values are calculated using a bundled, offline subset of the USDA FoodData Central database. Gram conversions, macro allocations, and caloric densities are resolved locally without network latency or external tracking.

warning Important Medical Disclaimer

LifeLens is explicitly a personal behavioral productivity and nutrition analytics tool, NOT a medical diagnostic system or healthcare advisory. The observations, correlation metrics, and focus-nutrition patterns are designed solely for self-reflection and cognitive pacing. They should never replace professional medical assessment, dietary consultation, or diagnosis.

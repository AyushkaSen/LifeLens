## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a balanced synthesis of structured editorial clarity and precision performance tracking—bridging the quiet intentionality of modern document workspaces with the analytical rigor of high-performance metabolic and productivity dashboards. 

### Emotional Response
- **Clarity & Equilibrium**: Users should experience an immediate drop in cognitive load. Information is abundant yet never noisy.
- **Quiet Authority**: AI-driven insights feel earned, measured, and trustworthy rather than predictive or speculative.
- **Deliberate Motion**: Micro-interactions are brief, crisp, and mechanistic, communicating operational efficiency.

### Design Movement & Archetype
The visual direction anchors in **Modern Precision Minimalist**, distinguished by:
- Soft off-white foundational canvases that prevent ocular fatigue during long analytical sessions.
- High-density information architecture organized through disciplined geometric grids and hairline structural boundaries.
- Precise, muted monochromatic hierarchy punctuated by focused botanical teals and targeted categorical chromatic accents for multi-variable data visualization.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-calm, analytical foundation with crisp contrast benchmarks compliant with WCAG AAA for text and AA for graphical interfaces.

### Role Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F766E`)**: Anchors critical actionable pathways, primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, and key metric focal points.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488`)**: Utilized for secondary interactive states, progress indicator fills, and supporting visual data anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#14B8A6`)**: Reserved for positive delta highlights, micro-trend markers, and focused hover accents.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Supplies structural boundaries, inactive states, secondary labels, and neutral graphical framing.

### Foundational Canvas & Slate Spectrum
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) provides the base ambient tone.
- **Surface Layer 1**: `#FFFFFF` for primary analytical cards and functional modules.
- **Surface Layer 2**: `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for inset wells, segmented control tracks, and table header rows.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Hairline `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for structure; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) for interactive control boundaries.
- **Text & Foreground**:
  - `High Contrast (Headings/Key Values)`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
  - `Body & Medium Contrast`: `#334155` (Slate 700)
  - `Muted / Metadata`: `#64748B` (Slate 500)
  - `Subtle / Placeholders`: `#94A3B8` (Slate 400)

### Data Visualization Palette
Used systematically across charts, macronutrient breakdowns, and productivity time-blocks:
- **Teal (Metabolic / Focus)**: `#0F766E`
- **Indigo (Deep Work / Cognitive Energy)**: `#6366F1`
- **Amber (Caloric / Metabolic Intake)**: `#F59E0B`
- **Rose (Fatigue / Stress Indicators)**: `#F43F5E`
- **Violet (Sleep / Circadian Rhythm)**: `#8B5CF6`

## Typography

The typographic system pairs three specialized typefaces to balance personality, readability, and technical precision:
1. **Hanken Grotesk**: Modern, architectural, and sharp. Applied across all primary headings, stat displays, and executive takeaways.
2. **Inter**: Neutral, hyper-legible, and system-tested. Applied to conversational text, long-form insights, inputs, and standard UI controls.
3. **JetBrains Mono**: Used exclusively for tabular data values, timestamps, nutritional metrics, and delta units to align numerical axes cleanly.

### Hierarchy & Typesetting Rules
- **Numerical Stacking**: When presenting large analytical metrics, pair `display` or `headline-lg` numbers in Hanken Grotesk with inline or trailing units rendered in `label-numeric` (JetBrains Mono) at 60% opacity.
- **Section Micro-Labels**: Use `label-caps` styled with `text-transform: uppercase` in `#64748B` to prefix chart cards, tables, and metric groups.
- **Tabular Alignment**: All numbers across tables and metric cards must use tabular figure settings (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point spatial grid with a **Fluid 12-Column Grid** tailored for desktop productivity dashboards and responsive mobile views.

### Form Factors & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid. Outer margin: `2rem`. Column gutter: `1.5rem`. Optimized for side-by-side analytical modules, persistent side navigation, and dual-pane workspace flows.
- **Tablet / Small Desktop (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid. Outer margin: `1.5rem`. Column gutter: `1rem`. Secondary analytical panels collapse into swipeable cards or tabbed structures.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column grid. Outer margin: `1rem`. Column gutter: `0.75rem`. Full-width card stacking; sidebars transition to drawer overlays.

### Architectural Canvas Rules
- **Container Limit**: Main desktop content containers max out at `1440px` to maintain optimal scan lines for continuous health charts.
- **Internal Module Spacing**: Component cards strictly enforce internal padding:
  - Dense widgets (Key-value pairs, tracker tags): `space-md` (`1rem`).
  - Standard analytical cards (Charts, tables, insight feeds): `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **flat-depth hybrid model** combining structural hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`), surface-container contrast, and diffused low-opacity ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, zero drop shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Surfaces)**: `#FFFFFF`. Outlined with a continuous 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Menus)**: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Insight Bars)**: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.

### Depth Principles
- No heavy directional drop shadows or pure black drops.
- Surface transitions (Level 1 to Level 2 on hover) translate `-1px` on the Y-axis with a 150ms ease-out curve.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a structured **Rounded** aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`), projecting modern software craftsmanship without leaning into childish or overly circular motifs.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Elements (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard buttons, input fields, dropdown menus, inline badges, and metric chips.
- **Containers & Cards (0.75rem - 1rem / 12px - 16px)**: Analytical cards, data tables, overview panels, and modal containers.
- **Micro-Indicators (0.25rem / 4px)**: Checkboxes, sparkline bars, timeline nodes, and progress pips.
- **Pill Shapes (9999px)**: Reserved solely for status tags (e.g., "Active", "Optimal", "High Protein") and continuous slider thumbs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, border none. Hover: `#0D9488`. Active: `#115E59`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost**: Background transparent, text `#334155`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Sizes**: 
  - Standard: `36px` height, horizontal padding `14px`, text `body-md` (medium weight).
  - Compact: `28px` height, horizontal padding `10px`, text `body-sm`.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Frame**: `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `12px` corner radius.
- **Header Structure**: Top-aligned title in `headline-sm` with contextual metadata or time filter chips in right alignment.
- **Padding**: `20px` to `24px` internal padding for data clarity.

### Chips & Badges
- **Metric Badges**: Soft tinted backgrounds (10% tint of target token) with solid token text. Example (Teal status): `#F0FDFA` fill, `1px solid #CCFBF1`, `#0F766E` text.
- **Categorical Chips**: `24px` height, `JetBrains Mono` at `11px`, `9999px` radius, providing rapid classification without competing with card titles.

### Input Fields
- **Container**: `36px` height, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `8px`, text `#0F172A` in `body-md`.
- **States**: 
  - Focus: Border color `#0F766E`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.12)`.
  - Placeholder: `#94A3B8`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: `16px x 16px`, `4px` radius, `#E2E8F0` border. Checked state: `#0F766E` background with white check icon.
- **Segmented Controls (Time / View Switchers)**: Outer well `#F1F5F9` with `6px` padding. Active tab: `#FFFFFF` surface with Level 1 shadow and `#0F172A` text.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Macro Distribution Bar**: Segmented horizontal progress track (`6px` height, `4px` radius) distributing protein, carbs, and fat via Teal, Amber, and Indigo tokens.
- **AI Insight Callout**: Border-left callout card with `3px solid #0F766E`, background `#F8FAFC`, featuring a subtle spark icon and an analytical takeaway in `body-md`.
- **Metric Stat Block**: Compact module with `label-caps` title, large `headline-lg` numeric value, and an inline trend badge (`+4.2%` in `#0D9488` with upward micro-arrow).
